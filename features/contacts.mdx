Every customer who chats, submits feedback, or books an appointment is automatically added to your Contacts list. Chatr enriches each contact with an AI-generated summary so you always know who you're talking to and what they care about.

## Where contacts come from

Chatr creates a contact record from three sources:

- **Chats** — Any visitor who sends a message and provides their email address during the conversation.
- **Feedback** — Visitors who submit a feature request, bug report, or general feedback through the widget.
- **Appointments** — Visitors who book a meeting through the widget's built-in scheduler.

You don't need to import or manually create contacts — Chatr builds the list automatically as visitors engage.

## Contact details

Open any contact to see a full activity snapshot:

- **Name and email address**
- **Last activity timestamp** — When the contact last interacted with the widget
- **Activity counts** — The number of chats, feedback submissions, and appointments on record
- **Linked records** — Direct links to individual conversation threads, feedback items, and appointments associated with that contact

## AI contact summary

The **Know Your Customers** panel appears on every contact page. Chatr generates a short AI summary covering:

- Their role and company
- What they've asked about or purchased
- Key concerns or requests they've raised
- Recommended follow-up actions

Use this summary to prepare for a call, craft a personalized reply, or hand context off to a teammate — without reading every message thread.

<Note>
  The AI summary updates automatically after each new conversation, feedback submission, or appointment.
</Note>

## Exporting contacts

Click **Download CSV** to export your full contact list. The export includes each contact's name, email, activity counts (chats, feedback, appointments), and last activity date. Use the export to import contacts into a CRM, build a segment in your email marketing tool, or share the list with your team.

## Filtering contacts

Use the filter controls above the contacts list to narrow your view:

- **Recent Activity** — Sorts contacts by most recent interaction, so your most active customers appear first.
- **All Sources** — Shows the complete list across all channels with no date-based filter applied.
