The Chatr Inbox is your team's command center for every customer conversation. It surfaces AI-handled threads, priority tickets, escalations, and unread messages in one organized view — with AI-suggested replies ready to send in one click.

## Conversation views

The Inbox is divided into tabs so you can zero in on exactly the conversations that need your attention.

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----------|---------------|
| **All** | Every conversation across all channels |
| **Unread** | Conversations with new messages you haven't seen |
| **Priority** | High-priority conversations flagged by the AI |
| **Assigned** | Conversations assigned to a specific team member |
| **Tickets** | Support tickets with a `#TKT-XXXX` reference number |
| **AI** | Conversations currently being handled autonomously by the AI |
| **Escalated** | Conversations the AI has flagged for human review |
| **Archived** | Closed or resolved conversations |

Switch between tabs to filter the list instantly. The count badge on each tab updates in real time as new messages arrive.

## AI-suggested replies

For every open conversation, the AI analyzes the full thread and generates a context-aware reply suggestion you can act on immediately. From the suggestion panel you can:

- **Accept and send** — Click the suggestion to insert it, then hit **Send** to deliver it in one click.
- **Edit before sending** — Modify the suggested text in the compose box before you send.
- **Insert template** — Click **Insert template** to pull in a saved response template and customize it for the conversation.

The AI bases its suggestions on your site content, previous messages in the thread, and the visitor's contact history — so suggestions stay on-brand and on-topic.

## Sending a reply

The compose box supports two reply modes. Toggle between them using the buttons above the text field:

- **Chat** — Sends the message via the widget back to the visitor in real time.
- **Email** — Sends the message to the visitor's email address as a standard email.

<Tip>
  Toggle to Email mode when a visitor has left the chat but you still need to follow up. Chatr captures their email automatically when they book or submit feedback.
</Tip>

## Ticket management

Every conversation in the Inbox is assigned a unique ticket number in the format `#TKT-XXXX`. Tickets carry one of two statuses:

- **Open** — The conversation is active and awaiting a response or resolution.
- **Closed** — The conversation has been resolved and is no longer active.

To escalate a conversation, click **Escalate** inside the thread. Escalating pauses AI handling immediately and notifies your team so a human can take over. Escalated conversations appear in the **Escalated** tab for easy triage. To resume AI handling, click **Take over** to claim the conversation yourself, or close it once resolved.
