Chatr's Feedback Hub captures what your customers actually want — directly through the chat widget. Every feature request, bug report, and piece of feedback lands in a structured Kanban board where your team can review, prioritize, and ship from.

## Feedback types

Chatr organizes incoming feedback into three categories:

- **Feature requests** — Ideas and suggestions from customers for new functionality they want to see in your product.
- **Bug reports** — Issues or errors customers have encountered while using your product.
- **General feedback** — Praise, concerns, or open-ended thoughts that don't fit neatly into the other two categories.

## The Feedback board

Feedback items appear as cards on a Kanban board, organized by type and status. From the board you can:

- **Review submissions** — Read each card to understand exactly what the customer reported.
- **See upvotes** — Customers can upvote existing requests, so the cards with the most votes rise to the top and show you which requests matter most to your audience.
- **Move cards between columns** — Drag cards across status columns (for example, **Under Review**, **Planned**, **Done**) to reflect where each item stands in your workflow.

## Public roadmap

Make your Feedback board public to give customers direct visibility into what your team is working on. A public board:

- Reduces repeat requests by showing customers a feature is already planned
- Builds trust by demonstrating that their feedback drives real decisions
- Keeps your community engaged as cards move from **Planned** to **Done**

Toggle the board's visibility in your Feedback settings.

## How visitors submit feedback

Customers submit feedback without ever leaving your site:

1. The visitor clicks the **Request a feature**, **Report a bug**, or **Share feedback** action card in the widget.
2. They choose a feedback type and describe their request in a short text field.
3. They submit — the card appears on your Feedback board instantly.

No separate form, no new page, no friction.

<Tip>
  Pair the Feedback Hub with the Changelog to close the loop: when you ship a requested feature, post an update so customers know their feedback was heard.
</Tip>
