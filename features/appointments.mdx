Chatr's Appointments feature turns your chat widget into a full booking system. Visitors can schedule calls, demos, or meetings without leaving the chat — and every booking appears in your Appointments dashboard with the visitor's contact details.

## How bookings work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visitor requests a booking">
    A visitor asks to book a call or clicks the **Book demo** action card in the widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI presents available slots">
    The AI displays available time slots based on your calendar settings, showing only times within your configured availability window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visitor confirms">
    The visitor selects a time and confirms — the booking is created instantly with no back-and-forth required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Both parties receive confirmation">
    You and the visitor receive confirmation details, including the meeting link (Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appointment appears in your dashboard">
    The new appointment shows up immediately in your Appointments dashboard, ready to manage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Appointments dashboard

The Appointments dashboard gives you two ways to view your bookings:

- **List view** — All appointments sorted by upcoming date. Each row displays the visitor's name, email, scheduled time, and a status badge: `scheduled`, `confirmed`, or `past`.
- **Calendar view** — A monthly calendar showing how many appointments fall on each day. Click any date to see the bookings for that day.
- **Archive** — Past appointments you've manually moved out of your active list. Use **Archive past** to bulk-archive all appointments whose time has passed.

## Appointment statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Scheduled** | The visitor booked a slot; your team hasn't confirmed it yet |
| **Confirmed** | Your team has confirmed the appointment |
| **Past** | The appointment time has passed |

## Setting your availability

Configure your meeting hours in **Widget Settings → Meeting availability**. Set your:

- **Start and end hours** — The earliest and latest times visitors can book
- **Days of the week** — Which days appear as available in the booking flow
- **Timezone** — Your local timezone so slots display correctly for your team

The AI respects these settings and only shows visitors slots that fall within your availability window. Times outside your window are never offered.

<Tip>
  Connect a meeting link (Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams) in **Integrations** so the AI automatically includes the join URL in the booking confirmation sent to you and your visitor.
</Tip>
