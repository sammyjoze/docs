Chatr Analytics gives you a real-time view of how your chat widget is performing — from how many conversations are happening to how many visitors become leads, book meetings, or complete in-chat purchases.

## Key metrics

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Conversations" icon="messages">
    Total chat sessions started in the selected time period.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Conversion rate" icon="trending-up">
    Percentage of conversations that result in a lead captured, booking made, or purchase completed.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Avg response time" icon="clock">
    How quickly the AI responds to incoming messages on average.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Revenue" icon="currency-dollar">
    Total in-chat Stripe revenue collected during the selected period.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Pipeline by stage

The pipeline funnel shows how visitors move from first message to conversion. Each stage tells you exactly where visitors are engaging — and where they drop off.

| Stage | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Leads captured** | Visitors who provided their contact information |
| **Appointments requested** | Visitors who initiated a booking through the widget |
| **Pricing requests** | Visitors who asked about pricing |
| **Checkout** | Visitors who started a Stripe checkout session |
| **Account created** | Visitors who signed up for your product |

Compare stage counts to find bottlenecks. A high number of pricing requests with low checkouts, for example, signals a conversion gap worth investigating.

## Charts available

Use the three built-in charts to spot trends over time:

- **Conversations & Leads** — Overlays total chat volume and leads captured across the selected time period. Use this to see whether more conversations are translating into more leads.
- **Bookings trend** — Shows scheduled demos and meetings over time. Useful for measuring how well your booking action card is performing.
- **Feedback mix** — A breakdown of feedback submissions by type: Feature requests (45%), Bug reports (30%), and General feedback (25%). Use this to understand whether customers are asking for new features or flagging problems.

## Time range

Use the date range selector at the top of the Analytics dashboard to choose your reporting window — for example, **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**. All metrics, pipeline numbers, and charts update instantly when you change the range.

<Tip>
  Track your Conversion rate weekly. A rate above 30% usually means the bot is well-trained and the action cards are relevant to your visitors.
</Tip>
