Chatr is designed to go from signup to a fully live AI assistant in under 2 minutes. Paste your URL, customize your bot, embed one script tag — and you're done. No engineering work, no manual training, no complicated setup. Follow the steps below to launch your widget.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [chatrai.app/signup](https://chatrai.app/signup) and create your account. Choose passwordless sign-in (enter your email and confirm with the 8-digit code sent to your inbox) or sign up with Google in one click. Every new account starts a **7-day free trial** — no credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your website URL">
    Enter your website address in the Chatbot Setup panel. Chatr crawls all pages of your site and trains the AI automatically — your products, pricing, FAQs, tone of voice, and policies are all absorbed without any manual input. When the crawl completes, you'll see a summary of everything the bot learned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your bot">
    Make the widget feel like your product. In the Chatbot Setup panel, set:

    - **Name** — give your assistant a persona that fits your brand
    - **Avatar** — upload a custom image, generate one, or choose a preset
    - **Primary color** — match your site's brand color
    - **Welcome message** — write the first thing visitors see when they open the chat
    - **Quick-reply suggestions** — add shortcut buttons that guide users to common actions
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable features">
    Head to the **Integrations** panel and toggle on the capabilities you want:

    - **Stripe payments** — connect your Stripe account to accept in-chat checkout
    - **Appointment booking** — link Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams to enable scheduling
    - **Feedback collection** — activate the Kanban board to capture bugs and feature requests
    - **GitHub changelog** — connect your repo to auto-generate release notes for users

    Each feature can be toggled on or off at any time — no redeployment required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Embed the widget">
    Copy the one-line script tag from the **Embed** section of your dashboard:

    ```html
    <script src="https://chatrai.app/widget.js" data-id="YOUR_WORKSPACE_ID" async></script>
    ```

    Paste it before the closing `</body>` tag on your site. The widget goes live the moment visitors load your page — no build step, no framework dependency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Your AI assistant is now running 24/7 — answering support questions, booking meetings, collecting feedback, and processing Stripe payments while you sleep. Check your **Inbox** to review conversations, and open **Analytics** to track message volume and top questions from day one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The AI updates automatically when you refresh your site scan. On the Scale plan, daily auto-scanning keeps the bot current with every site change.
</Note>
