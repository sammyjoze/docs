Chatr offers a suite of free AI chat tools that let you have a conversation with any content — whether it's a website, a PDF, a Word document, or plain text. No account required, no time limit.

## Available chat tools

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chat with Text" href="https://chatrai.app/tools/ai-chat-text-data">
    Paste any text and ask the AI questions. The AI answers only from the content you provide.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Chat with a Website" href="https://chatrai.app/tools/ai-chat-website-data">
    Enter a URL, load the page content, and chat with your site data.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Chat with a Document" href="https://chatrai.app/tools/ai-chat-document-data">
    Upload a `.txt`, `.md`, `.csv`, or `.json` file and ask questions about it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Chat with a PDF" href="https://chatrai.app/tools/ai-chat-pdf-data">
    Upload a PDF, extract the text, and ask questions about the document.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Chat with a Word Doc" href="https://chatrai.app/tools/ai-chat-word-data">
    Upload a `.docx` Word file and chat with its contents.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tool">
    Go to [chatrai.app/tools](https://chatrai.app/tools) and select the chat tool that matches your content type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your content">
    Paste your text, enter a URL, or upload your file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your question">
    Type your question in the chat box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a grounded answer">
    Get an AI answer grounded only in the content you provided.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  These tools are completely free and require no account. The AI answers only from the content you provide — it doesn't pull from the web or hallucinate information.
</Note>

## Use cases

- Summarize long documents quickly
- Extract key data points from reports
- Understand competitor content by chatting with their website
- Answer questions from internal docs without reading the whole file
- Analyze CSV or JSON data with natural language questions
