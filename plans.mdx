Start any plan free for 7 days with no credit card required.

## Payment & billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Chatr accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) as well as PayPal — all processed securely through Stripe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When does billing start?">
    Every plan begins with a **7-day free trial** — no credit card required at signup. When your trial ends (or if you choose to upgrade sooner), billing activates instantly at checkout and your plan unlocks all included features right away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my card data stored on Chatr servers?">
    No. All payments are processed by Stripe using industry-standard encryption. Chatr never stores your full card details on its own servers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    If something feels off, reach out to [team@chatrai.app](mailto:team@chatrai.app?subject=Chatr%20billing%20question) within 24 hours on business days and the team will help you sort it out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are prices inclusive of tax?">
    No — all displayed prices are exclusive of tax. Depending on your region, additional local taxes may be collected at checkout.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
