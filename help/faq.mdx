Got questions about Chatr? Here are answers to the most common ones. If you need help beyond what's here, use the chat widget on [chatrai.app](https://chatrai.app) or email [team@chatrai.app](mailto:team@chatrai.app).

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How fast can I go live?">
    Under 2 minutes. Paste your URL, customize the bot, copy the embed snippet, and paste it on your site. Chatr handles the rest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the AI learn from my site?">
    Chatr crawls all publicly accessible pages on your site. The bot reads product descriptions, pricing, FAQs, blog posts, and more to answer visitor questions accurately. It won't answer things that aren't on your site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add additional text content to train the bot?">
    Yes. Add custom text content in the **Brain & Knowledge** panel to supplement what the bot learns from crawling your site. This is useful for internal policies, off-site knowledge, or anything you want the bot to know that isn't published on a web page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages does Chatr support?">
    Chatr supports 12+ languages out of the box. The bot automatically detects the visitor's language and responds in kind — no configuration required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can visitors pay or book inside the chat?">
    Yes. With the Stripe integration enabled, visitors can complete checkout without leaving the widget. With a meeting link configured, they can book appointments directly in chat — no redirects, no handoffs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I customize the chatbot appearance?">
    Go to **Chatbot Setup** in your dashboard. You can set the bot name, avatar, primary color, welcome message, launcher shape, and quick-reply suggestions. Changes apply instantly — no redeployment needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I remove the 'Powered by Chatr' branding?">
    Yes. Remove it with the **White Label** add-on for a one-time fee of $19.99.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you have a plan for agencies?">
    Yes. The **Scale** plan (up to 10 workspaces) is designed for agencies managing multiple clients. For larger needs, contact [team@chatrai.app](mailto:team@chatrai.app) for Enterprise pricing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add more messages per month?">
    Add the **Extra Messages** add-on: +5k messages/mo for $39/mo. You can also upgrade to a higher plan for a larger included monthly allowance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I hit my message limit?">
    Contact support at [team@chatrai.app](mailto:team@chatrai.app). You can add extra messages mid-month with the Extra Messages add-on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple team members access the dashboard?">
    Yes. All plans include unlimited team members. Invite your team from the **Settings** panel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my email address?">
    Email [team@chatrai.app](mailto:team@chatrai.app) with your request. Email changes require manual verification for security reasons.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
