If something isn't working as expected, this guide covers the most common issues and how to resolve them. For anything not covered here, reach out at [team@chatrai.app](mailto:team@chatrai.app).

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="The widget isn't appearing on my site">
    Work through these checks in order:

    1. Verify the embed snippet is pasted before the closing `</body>` tag.
    2. Check that the `data-workspace-id` in the snippet matches the ID shown in your **Installation** tab.
    3. If you use Domain Targeting, make sure your domain is whitelisted in the Domain Targeting settings.
    4. Disable browser extensions — ad blockers can silently block the widget script.
    5. Open your browser console and check for JavaScript errors that reference the Chatr script.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bot is giving outdated or incorrect answers">
    1. Trigger a manual refresh in the **Brain & Knowledge** panel to force a re-crawl of your site.
    2. Verify your site pages are publicly accessible — pages behind a login wall cannot be crawled.
    3. Check that the page containing the correct information is linked from somewhere on your site. Chatr follows links, not sitemaps.
    4. Update your **Bot Description** to clarify the bot's scope if it's answering questions outside its intended domain.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bot isn't answering questions about a specific page">
    1. Ensure the page is linked from another page that Chatr has already crawled — Chatr follows links, not sitemaps.
    2. Check that the page content is in plain HTML text. Content loaded by JavaScript after the initial page render may not be captured.
    3. After fixing either issue, trigger a manual refresh in the **Brain & Knowledge** panel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stripe checkout isn't working">
    1. Verify your Stripe account is connected in **Integrations**.
    2. Confirm you've selected **In-chat Stripe checkout** — not **Link to pricing page**.
    3. Check that your Stripe Price IDs are entered correctly in the plan cards setup.
    4. Test in Stripe's test mode before going live to isolate configuration issues.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Appointments aren't being booked">
    1. Check that a meeting link is configured in **Integrations → Meeting links**.
    2. Verify your availability hours are set in **Widget Settings**.
    3. Ensure the **Appointments** action card is toggled on in **Widget Layout** settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="GitHub commits aren't appearing in the changelog">
    1. Click **Sync Now** in the GitHub integration panel to force an immediate sync.
    2. Verify the correct repository and branch are selected in the integration settings.
    3. Check that your GitHub account still has access to the repository.
    4. Re-authorize the GitHub connection if it was recently revoked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving feedback from visitors">
    1. Ensure the **Feedback** action card is toggled on in **Widget Layout** settings.
    2. Verify your AI suggestions include a prompt that encourages visitors to share feedback.
    3. Check the **Feedback** board in your dashboard — submissions appear there, not in the Inbox.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My message count seems wrong">
    - Message counts update in real time in the **Analytics** dashboard.
    - Each visitor message sent to the bot counts as one message toward your monthly limit.
    - AI-suggested replies sent by your team do **not** count toward the limit.
    - If you believe there is an accounting error, contact [team@chatrai.app](mailto:team@chatrai.app) with your workspace ID and the details.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  For issues not covered here, email [team@chatrai.app](mailto:team@chatrai.app). Include your workspace ID and a description of the issue — the team responds within 24 hours on business days.
</Note>
