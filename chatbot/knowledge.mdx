Chatr trains the AI by crawling your website. The bot reads every page it can find so it can answer questions about your products, pricing, features, and brand — without any manual copy-pasting. Give it a URL and it handles the rest.

## How training works

1. Paste your site URL in the **Brain & Knowledge** panel of the Chatr dashboard.
2. Chatr crawls all linked pages it can access from that root URL.
3. The AI learns your tone, products, pricing, FAQs, and guardrails from the content it finds.
4. The bot answers only from what it has crawled — it won't hallucinate information outside your site.

## Keeping the bot current

Your site changes over time, and the bot needs to keep up. Chatr offers several refresh frequencies depending on your plan:

| Frequency | How it works | Plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Manual refresh** | Click **Refresh** in the Brain & Knowledge panel to trigger a re-crawl immediately. | All plans |
| **Monthly auto-refresh** | Chatr automatically re-crawls your site once a month. | Starter, Growth |
| **Weekly auto-refresh** | Chatr automatically re-crawls your site once a week. | Scale, Enterprise |
| **Daily auto-scan** | The bot re-crawls every day so it stays current with every site change. | Scale, Enterprise |

<Note>
  After publishing new pricing or product pages, trigger a manual refresh so the bot immediately reflects the change — don't wait for the next scheduled scan.
</Note>

## What the bot knows

After a successful crawl, the bot has access to:

- All text content on publicly accessible pages
- Product descriptions, pricing tables, feature lists, FAQs, and blog posts
- The content in your **Bot Description** field, which is injected as brand context into every conversation

## What the bot doesn't know

Some content is outside what Chatr can crawl:

- **Password-protected or login-required pages** — The crawler can't authenticate, so private content is never included.
- **PDF or document files** — Unless the document's text is rendered as accessible HTML on a crawlable page, the bot won't see it.
- **Changes made after the last scan** — Always trigger a manual refresh after major site updates so the bot reflects the latest information.

<Tip>
  Keep a public FAQ or pricing page on your site. The more structured your site content, the more accurate the bot's answers will be.
</Tip>
