### Instant activation

Your Chatr plan unlocks as soon as checkout completes. Open the dashboard and start using every included feature right away.

### Security

Payments are processed by Stripe with industry-standard encryption. We do not store your full card details on Chatr servers.

### Money-back guarantee

If something feels off, we’ll help you sort it out fast. For billing questions, [contact us](mailto:team@chatrai.app?subject=Chatr%20billing%20question) anytime.

### Service support

Our team is here to help. Reach us by email or from the product—we aim to respond within 24 hours on business days.

Pricing is exclusive of taxes and additional local tax may be collected depending on your region.
