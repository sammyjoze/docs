### Stripe

Sell in chat, then send visitors to your pricing page or optional in-chat checkout

How visitors purchase

Default: AI explains plans, then visitors tap Get started to open your pricing or signup page. No chat sign-in required. In-chat Stripe is for advanced fulfillment workflows.

Link to pricing page

Recommended. Uses your existing checkout on your site.

In-chat Stripe checkout

Checkout inside the widget plus post-payment redirect or email.
