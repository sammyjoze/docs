Chatr's meeting integration attaches your video conferencing link to every appointment booked through the chat widget. When a visitor books a call, they automatically receive the join link — no manual follow-up needed.

## Supported providers

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Zoom" icon="video">
    Supports Personal Meeting Room links — a permanent URL tied to your account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Google Meet" icon="video">
    Supports any Meet room link generated from Google Calendar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Microsoft Teams" icon="video">
    Supports Teams meeting invite links from Calendar or Channel meetings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  **Coming soon:** Slack, Discord, and Zapier integrations are on the roadmap.
</Note>

## Setting up your meeting link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Integrations** in your Chatr dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your provider">
    Under **Meeting links**, select your provider (Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your meeting link">
    Paste your meeting room link into the field provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an appointment purpose">
    Set an **Appointment purpose** — a short description like "Discovery call" or "Live product demo". The AI uses this so it never has to guess what the meeting is for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save. Chatr will include this link in all booking confirmations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can only set one active meeting link at a time. If you run different call types, use the Appointment purpose field to describe the primary use case and handle any routing manually.
</Note>

## Finding your meeting link

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Zoom">
    Your Personal Meeting Room has a permanent ID and URL that doesn't change between sessions. To find it:

    1. Open the [Zoom web portal](https://zoom.us) and sign in.
    2. Go to **Meetings → Personal Room**.
    3. Click **Copy Invite Link**.

    The link follows the format `https://zoom.us/j/YOUR_PMI_NUMBER`.

    <Warning>
      If you reset your Personal Meeting Room passcode, the invite link may change. Update it in Chatr immediately to keep bookings working correctly.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google Meet">
    Google Meet links are tied to calendar events. To get a consistent link:

    1. Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) and create a new event.
    2. Click **Add Google Meet video conferencing**.
    3. Copy the Meet link from the event (e.g. `https://meet.google.com/xxx-xxxx-xxx`).

    <Tip>
      Use a recurring event so the link stays consistent across bookings without needing updates.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Microsoft Teams">
    Teams meeting links are generated from calendar invites. To find yours:

    1. Open [Microsoft Teams](https://teams.microsoft.com) and go to **Calendar**.
    2. Click **New Meeting** and fill in the details.
    3. Copy the **Join Microsoft Teams Meeting** link from the invite.

    <Tip>
      For a permanent room that doesn't change between sessions, use a **Channel meeting** link instead of a one-off invite.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Appointment purpose

The **Appointment purpose** field is shown to the AI assistant so it can accurately describe the meeting to visitors before they book. Keep it short and specific. Example values:

- "Discovery call"
- "Live product demo"
- "Technical onboarding session"
