Chatr's Stripe integration lets visitors complete purchases, subscribe to plans, and check out entirely within the chat widget. No redirect to an external checkout page required — the AI can present plan cards, answer pricing questions, and initiate payment in one continuous conversation.

## How Stripe works in Chatr

Chatr gives you two ways to handle purchases. Choose the mode that fits your existing checkout flow.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Link to Pricing Page">
    **Link to Pricing Page (Recommended)**

    - The AI explains your plans and answers pricing questions in chat.
    - When a visitor is ready to buy, it sends them to your existing pricing or signup page.
    - No Stripe account connection required for this mode — just paste your pricing page URL.
    - **Best for:** teams with an existing checkout flow who want the AI to handle pre-sales.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In-Chat Checkout">
    **In-Chat Stripe Checkout**

    - The full Stripe checkout appears inside the widget.
    - After payment, the visitor receives a confirmation or is redirected to a URL you specify.
    - Requires connecting your Stripe account in Integrations.
    - **Best for:** selling one-time products, subscriptions, or running payment-first flows.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Connecting Stripe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Integrations** in your Chatr dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Scroll to the **Stripe** section and click **Connect Stripe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Authorize Chatr to access your Stripe account via the Stripe OAuth flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the purchase method">
    Choose **In-chat Stripe checkout** as the purchase method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a fallback URL">
    Add your pricing or signup page URL as a fallback for visitors who prefer to check out on your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you only need the **Link to Pricing Page** mode, skip the Connect Stripe step and go straight to pasting your pricing URL — no Stripe account connection is needed.
</Note>

## Adding plan cards

Click **Add plan** in the Stripe integration panel to define the plans the AI can quote and display as cards in chat. For each plan, specify:

- **Plan name**
- **Price**
- **Key features** — a bullet list of what's included
- **Stripe Price ID** — found in your Stripe dashboard under **Products**

The AI will show these as interactive plan cards whenever visitors ask about pricing.

## Post-payment actions

After a successful checkout, configure either of the following:

- **Redirect URL** — Sends the visitor to a thank-you page or onboarding flow.
- **Email confirmation** — Sends a receipt or follow-up email to the buyer.

<Note>
  Chatr does not store any payment card data. All payment processing happens directly through Stripe's secure infrastructure.
</Note>

## Automate with webhooks

Need to trigger custom workflows after a payment — like provisioning accounts, updating your CRM, or notifying your team? Chatr supports **unlimited webhooks** so you can push payment and chat events to any external tool or API. Configure webhooks in your workspace Settings panel under **Integrations → Webhooks**.
