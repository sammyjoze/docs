Chatr is an all-in-one AI chat assistant that turns your website into a 24/7 sales and support engine. Instead of juggling half a dozen disconnected tools, you get one branded chat bubble that handles every customer touchpoint — answering support questions, booking meetings, collecting feedback, and closing deals with native Stripe checkout. Whether you run a SaaS product, an agency, or an e-commerce store, Chatr replaces the need for Intercom, Zendesk, Calendly, HubSpot, and Canny — at a fraction of the combined cost.

## Key features

- **AI trained on your entire site** — Chatr crawls every page of your website and learns your products, pricing, tone, and FAQs with zero manual entry.
- **In-chat Stripe checkout** — Accept payments and manage subscriptions inside the chat bubble without sending customers to a separate page.
- **Native appointment booking** — Integrate with Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams so visitors can book time directly from the conversation.
- **Feedback Kanban board** — Organize bugs and feature requests into a board with upvoting, status changes, and automatic email notifications to owners and users.
- **GitHub-powered changelog** — Connect your repo and Chatr auto-generates beautiful release notes from your commits, keeping users in the loop without lifting a finger.
- **Inbox with AI-assisted replies** — Every conversation lands in a shared inbox where your team can review threads, reply manually, or let the AI draft responses.
- **Contact CRM** — Every visitor interaction builds a contact record with conversation history, purchase activity, and lead data — no separate CRM required.
- **Analytics dashboard** — Track message volume, conversation outcomes, top questions, and team response times from one place.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste your URL — the AI learns your site">

  </Step>
